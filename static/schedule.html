Settings

Suggested bedtime

A marker on the Schedule tab's calendar, worked out from tomorrow's first commitment or scheduled task: that time, minus travel, minus your morning routine, minus a full night's sleep. It's advice, not a block -- it never takes up time or moves anything else.

Only fires while this app is open in a browser tab -- there's no background delivery, so this won't wake you up if the tab (or the computer) is closed.

Travel between locations

Each location's "Travel from home" above covers the first and last leg of a day -- home to a location and back. It can't express a trip between two locations directly, so add the pairs actually travelled here. A pair with no row falls back to going via home (deliberately pessimistic -- there are no coordinates in this system and no routing API; if that produces a silly number, add the pair instead of guessing at a distance). Travel is treated as symmetric by default, so adding a pair offers to save both directions at once.

Delete location

Its weekly hours, date overrides and any travel times involving it go too. Tasks that required it keep their other details -- they just lose the location.

Calendar import

From a file

A studio timetable exported as .ics. Events are matched on their calendar UID, so uploading the same (updated) file again updates existing commitments and removes any that are no longer in it, instead of duplicating everything.

From a feed URL

A live calendar feed. The URL is remembered after the first import, so re-syncing later is just Re-sync -- no need to paste it again.

Locations acquired on import

A feed session's site (A Building, Online, ...) becomes a location automatically the first time it's seen, so imported sessions get somewhere to be without hand-entry. A new one is filed under this umbrella -- travel is priced to and from here, and the specific room is still what shows on the calendar. Leave unset to have each site become its own umbrella instead.

Personal events

Going out, a haircut, a train -- time that's simply taken, not work to schedule. No estimate, nothing generated: a title, when, and optionally where. They're immovable, and may sit anywhere, including outside working hours.

Working & domestic hours

Two different weekly patterns: working hours are when you're willing to do project and university work; domestic hours are when chores happen. Both are separate from a location's own opening hours (under Locations) -- that's when a PLACE is open, this is when YOU are. Saving either one replans the schedule around the new pattern.
